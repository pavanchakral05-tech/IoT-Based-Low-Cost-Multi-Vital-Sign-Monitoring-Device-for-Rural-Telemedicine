# Abstract

## IoT-Based Low-Cost Multi-Vital Sign Monitoring Device for Rural Telemedicine

The project presents an IoT-based low-cost multi-vital sign monitoring device designed to support rural telemedicine and remote healthcare services.

The proposed system integrates biomedical sensors with an ESP32 microcontroller to monitor important physiological parameters, including heart rate, blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), electrocardiogram (ECG), and body temperature.

The MAX30100 sensor measures heart rate and oxygen saturation, the AD8232 module captures ECG signals, and the DS18B20 sensor measures body temperature. The collected data is processed by the ESP32 and displayed on an OLED display.

Using built-in Wi-Fi connectivity, the ESP32 transmits the collected health information to the ThingSpeak IoT cloud platform, where the data can be stored, visualized, and remotely accessed.

The device is designed to be affordable, portable, and suitable for academic demonstration of remote health monitoring, particularly in rural and underserved areas.

**Keywords:** Internet of Things, ESP32, Healthcare Monitoring, MAX30100, AD8232, DS18B20, ECG, SpO2, ThingSpeak, Rural Telemedicine.
